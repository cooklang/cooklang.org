A Recipe Markup Language

Cooklang is a simple text format for recipes that both cooks and computers understand. Get shopping lists, scaling and timers — from plain files you own forever.

Pancakes.cook
---
servings: 2
---
Crack the @eggs{3} into a #blender, add the @plain flour{125%g}, @milk{250%ml} and @sea salt{1%pinch}, and blitz until smooth.

Pour into a #bowl and leave to stand for ~{15%minutes}.

Melt the @butter{1%knob} in a #frying pan{} and cook each pancake for ~{2%minutes} a side.

Ingredients

2 servings
  • eggs3
  • milk250 ml
  • plain flour125 g
  • sea salt1 pinch
  • butter1 knob
blender bowl frying pan ⏱ 15 min ⏱ 2 min

Desktop app

Cook Editor — write recipes with AI on your side

A free, open-source desktop workspace for your .cook files: syntax highlighting, live preview, shopping lists, and meal plans — with CookBot, an optional AI assistant (part of Cook Pro, €12.99/mo) that imports recipes and drafts plans with your approval.

Explore Cook Editor
Cook Cloud

Optional cloud services for your recipes

Everything above works offline, with no account. When you want your recipes on every device, or help planning the week, Cook Cloud adds it — and your recipes stay plain .cook files you can take anywhere.

Sync

Write on the desktop, cook from your phone. One folder of .cook files kept in sync across Cook Editor, the mobile apps and the sync agent. Offline first. Sync comes with Cook Basic.

Advanced imports

Beyond web links: snap a cookbook page, drop a TikTok or Instagram link, or move your whole library from Paprika, Mela, Mealie and more. Everything arrives as a clean .cook file.

CookBot

Set a target — more protein, no gluten, a smaller shop. CookBot drafts the week from your own recipes and pantry, and shows every change as a diff you approve.

Nutrition & MCP

Nutrition numbers on recipes and plans, and an MCP server so the AI tools you already use can read your recipes.

Cooklang is built by one person since 2021. The language, parsers, CLI and Cook Editor are open source and stay that way — Cook Cloud subscriptions pay for the servers, the AI compute, and the time to keep the free tools free.
