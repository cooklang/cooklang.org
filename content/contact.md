## Community

- **Discord** — chat with the community and the maintainers: [discord.gg/fUVVvUzEEK](https://discord.gg/fUVVvUzEEK)

## Report Issues & Contribute

Cooklang is open source. Bug reports, feature requests, and pull requests are all welcome:

- **GitHub** — code, issues, and specification discussions: [github.com/cooklang](https://github.com/cooklang)
- **Spec discussions** — propose or debate language changes: [github.com/cooklang/spec/discussions](https://github.com/cooklang/spec/discussions)
