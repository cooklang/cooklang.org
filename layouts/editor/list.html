Meet CookBot, your AI sous-chef

A built-in AI sidebar that works directly on your recipe files — and always asks for your approval before it changes anything.

  • Import recipes from pasted text or a link, converted into clean Cooklang.
  • Draft meal plans from what you already have in your collection.
  • Edit across your library — tidy metadata, fix units, restructure steps — with every change shown for review first.

CookBot is the AI in Cook Editor and is part of Cook Pro — €12.99/month with a 7-day free trial, cancel anytime. Cook Editor itself is free and open source; everything above is part of Cook Pro. Plans and the trial live on Cook Editor at cook.md.

Cook Basic & Pro

Nutrition reports for any plan

Turn a .menu meal plan or a single recipe into a nutrition report: calories, protein, carbs, sugars and fat for the whole plan, per person per day, and per ingredient.

  • A plain-text template in your recipe folder, so you reuse it on every plan and change it however you like.
  • Ingredients already scaled to the servings in your plan, so the numbers match what you'll cook.
  • Rather not write a template? CookBot can write and fix it for you with Cook Pro.

Nutrition lookups use Cook's nutrition database, included with Cook Basic and Cook Pro.

Plugins

Make it yours with plugins

Plugins add panels, buttons in the recipe preview, commands, even new file types. Cook Editor runs the same kind of extensions as VS Code, plus a small Cooklang API, so a plugin works with your recipes the way the built-in features do.

  • Install in one click from the Extensions view, which searches plugins.cook.md, the Cook plugin marketplace.
  • Shopping List and Recipe Hub come built in; add Pantry, Nutri-Score, Allergens, a Meal Journal and more.
  • Build your own in an afternoon with a little TypeScript. The tutorial goes from an empty folder to a published plugin.
